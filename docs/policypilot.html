PolicyPilot AI

Enterprise Insurance Claims Decision Intelligence Platform

Overview

AI-powered claims adjudication platform that automates coverage determination across 10,000+ policy clauses using RAG, knowledge graphs, rule engines, and multi-agent workflows. Includes a document intelligence pipeline for OCR, clause extraction, semantic indexing, and real-time retrieval across 100,000+ insurance documents.

StackFastAPI · LangGraph · FAISS · Ollama/Llama · RAG · Knowledge Graphs · Multi-Agent
