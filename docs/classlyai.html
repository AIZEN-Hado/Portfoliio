ClasslyAI

AI-Powered LMS & Course Marketplace

Overview

A modern Learning Management System combining teaching, course delivery, marketplace sales, student engagement, and AI-assisted learning. Students browse, purchase (Stripe) and learn; instructors publish and analyze; admins monitor the platform — with AI tools for notes, quizzes, and answer checking.

StackReact 19 · Vite · Tailwind CSS · Node.js · Express · MongoDB · Stripe · Google OAuth
